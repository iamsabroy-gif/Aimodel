Tea is a drink made by pouring hot water over the leaves of the tea plant, Camellia sinensis. It is the most widely consumed drink in the world after water. Tea was first used in China, where legend says that Emperor Shennong discovered it around 2737 BC. The main types are black, green, white and oolong tea, and all of them come from the same plant. Black tea is fully oxidized, while green tea is dried without oxidation. Tea contains caffeine, but less than coffee does. India and China are the largest producers of tea, and Kenya is the largest exporter of black tea. Tea plants grow best in warm, wet climates on hillsides. In Britain, people often drink tea with milk, and the afternoon tea tradition began in the 1840s. Japan is famous for matcha, a powdered green tea used in its tea ceremony.
